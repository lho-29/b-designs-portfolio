MOMENT

The design identity of Moment Parfum reflects timeless elegance and refined luxury through a cohesive visual system. The warm brown and gold palette evokes sophistication and intimacy, while the minimalist logo and poetic tagline establish a premium aura. Each mockup—from boutique storefronts to packaging, stationery, and digital displays—demonstrates consistency and attention to detail, ensuring the brand resonates across physical and digital touchpoints. This approach highlights how design can elevate a perfume brand into an immersive experience, where every element embodies exclusivity, artistry, and emotional connection.
